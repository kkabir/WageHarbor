# WageHarbor launch kit

Ready-to-paste copy for listing, outreach and ads. Before using any of it: HTTPS must be live on www.wageharbor.com, the support mailbox in `Site:ContactEmail` must exist, and Privacy/Terms should have had a legal review.

## Launch checklist

- [ ] SSL certificate active; set `Https:Enabled` to `true` in `appsettings.Production.json` and republish.
- [ ] `Site:ContactEmail` mailbox exists and is monitored.
- [ ] Privacy policy and Terms reviewed by a lawyer (add governing law and your business entity name).
- [ ] Google Search Console: verify www.wageharbor.com, submit `https://www.wageharbor.com/sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Analytics: create a Plausible site for `wageharbor.com`, set `Analytics:PlausibleDomain`.
- [ ] Listings: Capterra, G2, GetApp, SaaSworthy, Product Hunt (copy below).
- [ ] Record the 2-minute demo video (script below) and add it to YouTube and LinkedIn.
- [ ] Weekly numbers: visitors, company sign-ups, companies that ran a first payroll.

## Short descriptions

**Tagline (60 chars):** Free payroll & HR for small business. Pay people right.

**One sentence:** WageHarbor is free payroll and HR software for US small businesses: automatic payroll taxes, direct deposit, printable pay stubs, a time clock and time off, with a login for every employee.

**Listing description (~150 words):**
> WageHarbor helps small businesses pay their people correctly and on time, without expensive software. Register your company in two minutes, add your employees, and run payroll in three steps: calculate, approve, pay. WageHarbor works out federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, state withholding, FUTA and SUTA for every paycheck, creates a NACHA direct-deposit file for your bank, and produces the tax liability, Form 941 worksheet, W-2 and 1099 reports your accountant needs.
>
> Every employee gets their own login to view and print pay stubs, clock in and out, request time off and update their W-4. Managers approve time from the same place. Social Security and bank numbers are encrypted, each company's data is kept separate, and roles control who sees what.
>
> WageHarbor is free for small businesses. It calculates payroll taxes; you file and pay them.

**Categories:** Payroll software · HR software · Time tracking · Small business
