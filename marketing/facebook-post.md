## Keep it accurate
- WageHarbor **calculates** payroll taxes; companies **file and pay** them. Don't say "we file your taxes".
- Pricing: free for 1 person on payroll, then $12/month + $1.50 per person. Compare only with *published* prices (Gusto Simple / OnPay: $49 + $6 per person, November 2026), and say "about ¼", not an exact figure.
- The pay stub on the image is the Harborlight Coffee demo company, not a real customer.
